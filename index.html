01 / ABOUT＋

Sound is more
than a signal.

Understanding what we hear.
Shaping how we talk to AI.

At Tencent Hunyuan, my current work centers on audio understanding and voice conversations: developing audio language models for speech and general sound understanding, and enabling AI to communicate through voice. I’m based at Tencent America in Bellevue, Washington.

My graduate research at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign’s SiNRG Lab was advised by Prof. Romit Roy Choudhury. I also collaborated with Prof. Mark Hasegawa-Johnson on speech research.

I’m especially interested in connecting generative priors with signal processing: from diffusion-based speech enhancement and separation to neural codecs, spatial audio, and music generation.
